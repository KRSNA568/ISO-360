# Free ISO 27001:2022 toolkit

Templates from https://27001certified.in/iso-27001/toolkit — free to use, adapt and
share inside your organisation or with your clients. No attribution required, no signup.

## Files

- `statement-of-applicability.csv` — Statement of Applicability. All 93 Annex A controls, pre-filled and ready to justify. (6 kB)
- `risk-register.csv` — Risk register. Inherent score, treatment, residual score — the whole chain in one row. (2 kB)
- `internal-audit-checklist.csv` — Internal audit checklist. Every clause requirement as an audit question, with the evidence to ask for. (14 kB)
- `gap-assessment.csv` — Gap assessment. Where you stand against the whole standard, before you commit to a date. (35 kB)
- `nonconformity-corrective-action-log.csv` — Nonconformity & corrective action log. Root cause and an effectiveness check, not just a tick in a box. (2 kB)
- `management-review-agenda.md` — Management review agenda. Every Clause 9.3 input as an agenda item, with who brings what. (5 kB)

## Before you start

The CSV files open in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Numbers. Save them in your
own format once opened, so formulas and formatting survive.

Suggested order: gap assessment first to see where you stand, then the risk register,
then the Statement of Applicability (applicability follows from risk), then the audit
checklist, corrective action log and management review agenda as the ISMS starts running.
